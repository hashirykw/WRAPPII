Ask us directly
How much does PPF cost?

Full-car PPF is NOK 30,000 for small cars, 33,000 for mid-size, 36,000 for large cars and SUVs and from 45,000 for XL cars and vans. Front PPF starts at 12,900.

What does Color PPF cost?

In the autumn campaign: car from NOK 33,000, SUV from 38,000 and van from 45,000, with a 10-year warranty. We're booking for November and December.

Why are wraps "price on request"?

The price of a colour-change wrap, chrome delete, stripes or fleet graphics depends on the model and the design, so we price it after we've seen the car.

Do you work on electric cars?

Yes. Most of the cars on our price list are electric, from the ID.3 and IONIQ 5 to the Model Y and ID. Buzz.
