Ask us directly
Which films do you wrap with?

We wrap with 3M Wrap Film Series 2080, Avery Dennison Supreme Wrapping Film and Hexis. You can try the 3M and Avery colours on a car in our 3D studio.

How much does a wrap cost?

Colour changes, chrome delete, stripes and fleet graphics are priced after we've seen the car, because the price depends on the model and the design. Send us the car and what you want on WhatsApp.

Are the colours on screen exact?

They're a guide. Screens show colour differently, and metallic, pearl and flip films change with the light. We show you the real film sample in the studio before we start.

What's the difference between a wrap and Color PPF?

A wrap changes the colour with vinyl film. Color PPF changes the colour with a thicker, self-healing protection film, so you get the new colour and paint protection in one.

Can you put our company logo on the car?

Yes. We've done vans and trucks like the DHL service-partner van and Akershus Logistikk's trucks. Send us your logo and the vehicle.
