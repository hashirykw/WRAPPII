About us · Wrappi – Wrapping Art Design

The people
behind Wrappi

Passion for cars.An eye for detail.Quality in every single project.

Behind Wrappi – Wrapping Art Design are the two of us, Joanna Gonkiewicz and Anestis Lekas. Together we've built a business where creativity, precision and personal service are at the centre.

We believe a car is more than just a way to get around. It reflects its owner's personality, style and wishes. That's why we treat every single car with the same care and respect as if it were our own.

Two people, one studio

Two roles.
One result.

Anestis brings the craft and the precision. Joanna brings the creativity and the customer contact.

AL
Craft and precision

Anestis Lekas

Anestis is the practical and technical driving force behind Wrappi. With an eye for detail, patience and high standards for quality, he makes sure every project is carried out with the greatest possible precision.

For him, car wrapping isn't just about applying film — it's about creating a result that both looks fantastic and lasts.

  • Eye for detail
  • Patience
  • High standards
JG
Creativity and customer contact

Joanna Gonkiewicz

Joanna is behind much of the planning, organisation and development of Wrappi. She's passionate about design, aesthetics and great customer experiences.

From the first conversation until the car is ready for delivery, her goal is for every customer to feel confident, looked after and happy with both the process and the result.

  • Design
  • Aesthetics
  • Customer experience
Our philosophy

It's not about doing as many cars as possible in the shortest possible time.

It's about doing the job properly.

We combine creativity with quality materials, thorough work and personal follow-up.
