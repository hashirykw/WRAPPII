Ask us directly
What's included in Full Shine?

Professional paint correction, a deep gloss finish with swirl removal, premium protection, complete interior detailing, and wheels and tyres detailed. NOK 5,999, or NOK 6,999 with ceramic coating.

What's the difference between 1-step and 2-step polishing?

1-step is paint enhancement, from NOK 2,990. 2-step is paint correction, from NOK 4,990.

Which ceramic coating do you use?

Koch Chemie. You get a certificate with checks at 12, 24 and 36 months.

Does ceramic coating stop stone chips?

No — that's what PPF is for. Ceramic coating adds deep gloss and makes the car easier to wash. Many owners combine the two.
