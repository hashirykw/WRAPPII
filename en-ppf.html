The process

Why 3–4 days
for PPF?

From the first message to the day you collect the car, in five steps.

01 / 05
01

Send us the car

WhatsApp or call with the model and what you want done. We give you a price and a date, and you drop the car at Strømsveien 324.

02

Fitted wet

PPF goes on wet, with a special mounting fluid. That gives us time to place the film exactly and get an even, precise result.

03

20–24 working hours

The fitting itself takes about 20–24 working hours, depending on the car and how much work it needs.

04

Settle and check

After fitting, the film needs around 24 hours to dry and settle. Then we check the edges, details and finish to make sure everything sits as it should.

05

Pick-up

Once you have the car back, we recommend not washing it for the first 48–72 hours.

Quality takes time. Proper curing gives a safer, longer-lasting result.

Ask us directly
Why do you need the car for 3–4 days for PPF?

PPF is fitted wet with a special mounting fluid so the film can be placed exactly. The fitting takes about 20–24 working hours, depending on the car. Then the film needs around 24 hours to dry and settle before we check the edges, details and finish.

Which PPF film do you use?

PPF Art Design Titanium: 216 µm in total, 165 µm of TPU, a 25 µm self-healing top coat, rated from −45 °C to +115 °C.

What does Front PPF cover?

The whole bonnet, front bumper, both front wings, both A-pillars, both side mirrors and the headlights. From NOK 12,900.

How much does PPF cost?

Full-car PPF is NOK 30,000 for small cars, 33,000 for mid-size, 36,000 for large cars and SUVs and from 45,000 for XL cars and vans. Front PPF starts at 12,900. Price depends on the model and design, so send us your car for an exact figure.

What's the difference between PPF and Color PPF?

Both are self-healing protection film. Clear PPF keeps your car's own colour; Color PPF changes the colour at the same time, while the original paint stays protected underneath.

What warranty do I get?

Up to 10 years' manufacturer warranty on both the film and the fitting.

When can I wash the car?

We recommend not washing it for the first 48–72 hours after you pick it up.

PPF or ceramic coating — which do I need?

They do different jobs. PPF is a physical film that takes stone chips and scratches. Ceramic coating adds deep gloss and makes the car easier to wash, but it won't stop a chip. Plenty of owners do both.
